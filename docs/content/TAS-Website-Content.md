- "Learn Today, Lead Tomorrow" — this is not ours. Delete it from every page, banner, meta tag and image alt text.

- The old prospectus admission form (retired).

**Do not advertise** (not offered): school transport, smart classrooms.
**Do not publish:** fees, 5-year board results, student names or photos without permission.

**Contact numbers:** copy exactly; never add or change a number.

## 3. About Us

### Our Journey

The Arqam Schools began in 1993 in Faisalabad, with a single campus and a clear purpose: to give children a sound modern education rooted in the Quran and Sunnah, and to earn the pleasure of Allah through the work of teaching.

As parents' trust grew, the school grew with it. From Faisalabad, The Arqam Schools expanded step by step to other cities, opening campuses in Lahore, Karachi and beyond, with head offices in both Faisalabad and Lahore.

Today, in 2026, The Arqam Schools is a network of more than 350 branches across Pakistan, educating over 250,000 students from Play Group to Matric, including more than 20,000 Hifz-e-Quran students. The purpose remains what it was in 1993: excellence in this world and excellence in the Hereafter.

### Name and its significance

"The Arqam" has special importance and unique grace in the history of Islam. It was the pioneer educational institution of the Muslim society, which came into existence under the leadership of Rasool Allah ﷺ. It was the basic school of Islamic thought, established at the residence of Hazrat Zaid Bin Arqam (R.A.), the well-known companion of the Holy Prophet ﷺ, with a view to building the character of the people coming into the circle of Islam. Keeping that first glorious Islamic educational academy in view, this institution has been named "The Arqam Schools". The basic aim of the school is to teach and train the present Muslim generation with the Islamic spirit. May Allah accept it and bless us with strength for betterment.

## 5. Academics

**Curriculum:** The Arqam Schools follows its own curriculum, approved by the Government of Pakistan education boards.

**Classes:** Play Group to Matric.

**Medium of instruction:** English, alongside Urdu.

**Quranic education** (taught alongside general education):
- **Hifz-e-Quran** — memorisation of the Holy Quran with experienced, devoted teachers.
- **Tajweed** — correct pronunciation and recitation of the Quran.
- **Nazra** — Quran reading for every student.
- **Islamic practice** — Namaz, Qira'at and the fundamentals of Islam, taught through easy methods and practised daily.

**Co-curricular activities:** Husn-e-Qira'at, Naat Khawani, morning assembly presentations, Urdu and English debates and speech competitions, art and craft, theme days and educational trips — all designed to build confidence and help students overcome shyness.
